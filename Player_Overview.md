### Hello and welcome to Joining Party overview for players.


Joining Party allow to expand the groupe of Charname to more than 6 members while preserving banters, interjections and quests.

let's get started :

At the beginning of the campaign (BG1, SoD, BG2 or ToB) Charname receive a special ability named "Engagement".

The ability will be used to change party members to travelers and to enable a PID. The PID is here to manage travelers.

There is two major ways to make travelers available as full party member. As mentionned in the readme.

---

- The Switching Party.

This options allow travelers to switch there place with actual party members to altern time in party and time as traveler.
With a full groupe of six party members, every custom time (can be configured with mod componants when wanted and changed while playing).
   - The switch can happen only with NPC integrated in the mod. ([Full list here]())
   - Players can choose if a traveler is allowed to switch.
   - Players can choose if a party member agree to switch.
   - NPC not integrated in the mod will always remain party member.
   
 This options is pretty safe and make sure that travelers will stay in party long enough to trigger whatever hey should.
 As traveler everything should als trigger as intended, but we never know.
 The option is also a mean to take the time to really manage travelers as party member.

---

- The Quick Switch.

This options allow travelers to join the group for a instant before returning to the traveler state.
It necessite to keep at least a free slot for travelers to come and go, every custom time (can be configured with mod componants when wanted and changed while playing).
    - The Quick switch is here to manage level up and inventory for traveler while preserving your core party and formation.


---


- Fill party is an option by default, that make traveler come in party if the party have less than tree members, it can be deactivated with Charname PID.


---


- The Loading Switch.

It's the more important part of the mod and will proceed each time a save is loaded.
A cutscene will quickly make each travelers come and leave the party to enable the capacity of interject, banter and be recognised as party member. (It's related to the game engine)
Having a full party of six is not a problem, the Loading Switch will manage everything.

It's mandatory and need to happen. An option to force the Loading Switch is available in PID, in case something as prevented it to launch at loading.


---

- The Switching Room.

An area similar at Candlekeep first floor, used to perform the Loading Switch when it cannot take place in the current area. (It's related to the game engine)
    - The Switching room is like a stronghold.
    - Players can rest.
    - Several containers are available.


#### Transitions between campaigns.


Between BG1 and SoD or SoD and BG2.
Every travelers will leave the group.
    - With Endless BG1, after asking to the dukes to skip SoD.
    - In Korlaz donjon after defeating Korlaz and before leaving the first area.
    - In the Sewers / Caverns at the end of SoD before being taken to BG2.

Depending of their content in next campaign, they will remain in the Switching Room or leave to proceed their story.
Each travelers will drop their gears in a container of the Switching room but will keep their equipement and weapons.

Travelers without content can be hired again after the beginning of the next campaign.
On contrary of NPC in party vanishing at the end of BG1 or SoD.

So if you want to keep Montaron in SoD make sure he is a traveler.
Same if you want to keep Indira, Valerie or other BG1 only mod NPC.
